The EMI score under five intensity denominators. Each estimate comes from a separate regression of log Scope 1+2 emissions per unit of the denominator on the EMI score and log market capitalisation, with country-group and sector fixed effects and standard errors clustered by country group; bars are 95 per cent confidence intervals. Sector-demeaned outcomes subtract the median of that intensity within the firm's sector and year. The shaded band holds the two physical denominators, and the number of firms in each regression is given on the right.
